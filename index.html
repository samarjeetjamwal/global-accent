Global Accent Translator - Help

Use this app to convert your speech to different English accents.

Instructions

  • Click 🎤 Start Recording to capture speech (best on Chrome/Edge).
  • Select input language and target accent.
  • Choose Local for offline conversion or Remote with your proxy server.
  • Click 🔄 Convert or double-click transcript to convert (Ctrl+S for sample).
  • Use voice settings to adjust rate, pitch, and volume.
  • Select a voice from the list and play original or accented audio.
  • View history to restore previous conversions.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: Ctrl+S (sample), Ctrl+C (copy), Ctrl+D (download).
